# Remote Work Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-RW-002 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-02-01

## 1. Work models

Aurora Dynamics operates a **hybrid-by-default** model:

- **Hybrid** employees work from their assigned office at least 2 days per week.
- **Remote** employees have no office attendance requirement. Remote status is
  granted at hire or by written approval from the employee's manager and
  department head.
- **On-site** roles (lab, facilities, front desk) are designated in the job
  description and are not eligible for remote work.

## 2. Working from a different location temporarily

- Working from another location **within your country of employment** for up to
  **2 weeks per quarter** requires only manager notification.
- Working from another location within your country for **more than 2 weeks**
  requires manager approval in the HR portal.
- **Working from another state or province** for more than **30 consecutive
  days** requires People Operations approval, because it can create payroll tax
  and registration obligations for the company.

## 3. Working from another country

- Any cross-border work requires **advance approval from People Operations and
  Legal**, regardless of duration. Submit the request at least **3 weeks**
  before travel.
- Cross-border work is capped at **90 days per rolling 12 months**, subject to
  visa/work-authorization rules of the destination country.
- Employees must remain on their home-country payroll and must not sign
  contracts or conduct sales activity abroad (permanent-establishment risk).
- All company data-security rules apply when working abroad — see the Data
  Security Policy (POL-SEC-005), in particular the VPN and public-Wi-Fi rules.

## 4. Requirements while remote

- Maintain a reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps down / 5 Mbps up).
- Be reachable on Slack and email during your team's core hours
  (10:00–16:00 in your team's primary time zone).
- Attend the quarterly in-person team week; travel is booked as a business
  expense under the Expense Policy (POL-EXP-003).

## 5. Equipment and workspace

- The company issues one laptop per employee (see Equipment Policy
  POL-EQP-007).
- Remote employees receive a one-time **home-office stipend of $500** for
  furniture and peripherals, reimbursed under the Expense Policy rules.
- Company equipment abroad remains subject to export-control screening for
  trips longer than 30 days.

## 6. Revocation

Remote status may be revoked with 30 days' notice for documented performance
or business reasons.
