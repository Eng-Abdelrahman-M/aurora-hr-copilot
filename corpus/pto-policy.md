# PTO Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-PTO-001 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-01-15

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs paid time off (PTO) for all Aurora Dynamics employees.
Contractors and vendors are not covered by this policy; their time off is
governed by their individual service agreements.

## 2. Accrual

- **Full-time employees** accrue **1.667 vacation days per month** (20 days per
  year), starting on their first day of employment.
- **Part-time employees** (fewer than 30 hours/week) accrue vacation on a
  pro-rated basis relative to a 40-hour week.
- Employees with **5 or more years of service** accrue 2.083 days per month
  (25 days per year).
- Sick leave is granted as a lump sum of **10 days on January 1** each year and
  does not accrue monthly. New hires receive a pro-rated sick balance.

## 3. Requesting PTO

1. Submit the request through the HR portal **at least 10 business days before**
   the first day of leave for requests of 3 days or more.
2. Requests of 1–2 days require **3 business days'** notice.
3. All PTO requests require **manager approval** before they are considered
   confirmed. An unapproved request is not time off.
4. Same-day sick leave does not require advance notice; notify your manager
   before 10:00 local time and log it in the portal within 48 hours.

## 4. Approval rules

- Managers must respond to PTO requests within **3 business days**.
- Managers may decline requests only for documented business reasons
  (release freeze, coverage below minimum staffing).
- Requests during a declared **blackout period** (see section 6) require
  director-level approval in addition to manager approval.

## 5. Carryover and payout

- Up to **5 unused vacation days** carry over into the next calendar year.
  Carryover days must be used by **March 31** or they are forfeited.
- Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.
- Upon termination, accrued unused vacation is paid out where required by law.

## 6. Blackout periods

Department heads may declare blackout periods of up to 3 weeks per quarter
during critical launches. Blackout periods must be announced at least 30 days
in advance.

## 7. Negative balances

Employees may borrow up to **3 days** against future accrual with manager
approval. Larger advances require People Operations approval.
