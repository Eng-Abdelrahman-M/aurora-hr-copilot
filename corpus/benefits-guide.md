# Benefits Guide — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-BEN-004 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-01-05

## 1. Eligibility

- **Full-time employees** (30+ hours/week) are eligible for the full benefits
  package: medical, dental, vision, life insurance, and 401(k).
- **Part-time employees** (20–29 hours/week) are eligible for dental, vision,
  and 401(k), but **not** company-sponsored medical coverage.
- **Contractors are not eligible** for any company benefit plan.
- Coverage begins on the **first day of the month following the hire date**.

## 2. Medical, dental, vision

- Three medical plans are offered: Base PPO (company pays 80% of premium),
  HDHP with HSA (company pays 90% and contributes $1,000/year to the HSA),
  and Premium PPO (company pays 70%).
- Dental and vision premiums are covered at 100% for the employee and 50% for
  dependents.

## 3. Enrollment windows

- **New hires** have **30 days from their start date** to enroll. Missing the
  window means waiting for open enrollment.
- **Open enrollment** runs every year from **November 1 to November 15**,
  effective January 1.
- **Qualifying life events** (marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other
  coverage) open a **30-day special enrollment window** from the event date.
  Documentation is required.

## 4. 401(k)

- Eligible from day one; auto-enrollment at 4% unless declined.
- Company match: **100% of the first 4%** of salary contributed.
- Match vests immediately.

## 5. Life insurance and disability

- Company-paid life insurance at 1× annual salary for all benefits-eligible
  employees; supplemental coverage up to 4× available at employee cost.
- Short-term disability covers 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day
  waiting period.

## 6. Changing elections

Outside open enrollment and qualifying life events, elections cannot be
changed. Contact benefits@aurora.example for event verification.

## 7. Part-time and contractor coverage

Part-time employees contracted for 20 hours per week or more are eligible for
medical, dental and vision cover on the same terms as full-time employees;
the company contribution is pro-rated to contracted hours. Part-time employees
below 20 hours per week are eligible for the employee assistance programme and
the 401(k) plan but not for company-funded medical cover.

Contractors and agency staff are not enrolled in Aurora Dynamics benefits.
Where a contract states otherwise, the contract governs.

An employee whose contracted hours change across the 20-hour threshold has
their eligibility reassessed from the first of the following month, and this
counts as a qualifying life event for the purpose of changing elections
(section 6).

## 8. Dependants and life events

Spouses, registered domestic partners and children up to age 26 may be added
to medical, dental and vision cover. Documentation — a marriage or partnership
certificate, or a birth or adoption record — is required within 30 days of
adding a dependant.

Qualifying life events allow elections to be changed outside the annual
enrolment window: marriage or partnership, divorce or dissolution, birth or
adoption, a dependant losing other coverage, a change in employment status, or
a permanent relocation that changes the available plan network. The change
must be requested within 30 days of the event, and takes effect on the first
of the following month.

## 9. Employee assistance and wellbeing

All employees, including part-time and fixed-term, have access to the employee
assistance programme: confidential counselling, legal and financial guidance,
and 24-hour crisis support. Use of the programme is not visible to Aurora
Dynamics; the company receives only aggregate usage statistics with no names.

A wellbeing allowance of USD 500 per calendar year covers gym membership,
fitness classes, mental health apps and ergonomic accessories not already
provided as equipment. It is claimed through the expense system
[POL-EXP-003 section 1] and does not carry over between years.

## 10. Benefits while on leave

Medical, dental and vision cover continue unchanged during paid parental,
medical and bereavement leave. During unpaid personal leave of more than 30
days, cover continues for the first 90 days, after which the employee may
either pay the premium directly or suspend cover and re-enrol on return
without a waiting period. See the Leave of Absence Policy [POL-LOA-006 section
5].

401(k) contributions pause automatically during unpaid leave, since they are
deducted from salary, and resume with the first pay period after return.

## 11. Retirement plan details

Aurora Dynamics matches 401(k) contributions at 100% of the first 4% of
eligible pay and 50% of the next 2%, giving a maximum company contribution of
5% of pay. The match is calculated per pay period rather than annually, so an
employee who front-loads their contributions early in the year may receive
less match overall; those wishing to maximise the match should contribute
evenly across the year.

The match vests immediately. There is no cliff and no forfeiture on leaving,
which means an employee who leaves after one month keeps every matched dollar.

Employees may choose between a traditional pre-tax contribution and a Roth
after-tax contribution, or split between them. The plan administrator provides
individual guidance; Aurora Dynamics does not give financial advice, and
People Operations will not recommend an allocation.

Employees outside the United States are enrolled in the equivalent local
scheme where one exists, at a contribution level designed to be broadly
comparable. Details are confirmed in writing during onboarding
[POL-ONB-008 section 9].

## 12. Equity

Full-time employees receive a stock option grant on joining, with the size set
by role and level. Grants vest over four years with a one-year cliff: nothing
vests before the first anniversary, then 25% vests, and the remainder vests
monthly over the following three years.

Vesting pauses during unpaid leave longer than 90 days and resumes on return
[POL-LOA-006 section 8]. It continues normally during all paid leave,
including parental leave.

Leavers have 90 days from their final day to exercise vested options, extended
to twelve months where the employee has completed four years of service.
Unvested options are cancelled. The company does not repurchase vested shares.

Refresh grants are considered annually alongside the compensation review and
are not automatic.

## 13. What benefits do not cover

The benefits described here are the complete set. Aurora Dynamics does not
provide company cars, housing allowances, relocation payments beyond those
agreed in an individual offer, school fees, or private medical cover for
extended family beyond the dependants defined in section 8.

Where an employee believes a benefit should exist, the route is a suggestion
to People Operations during the annual benefits review each September, not an
expense claim.
