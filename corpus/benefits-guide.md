# Benefits Guide — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-BEN-004 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-01-05

## 1. Eligibility

- **Full-time employees** (30+ hours/week) are eligible for the full benefits
  package: medical, dental, vision, life insurance, and 401(k).
- **Part-time employees** (20–29 hours/week) are eligible for dental, vision,
  and 401(k), but **not** company-sponsored medical coverage.
- **Contractors are not eligible** for any company benefit plan.
- Coverage begins on the **first day of the month following the hire date**.

## 2. Medical, dental, vision

- Three medical plans are offered: Base PPO (company pays 80% of premium),
  HDHP with HSA (company pays 90% and contributes $1,000/year to the HSA),
  and Premium PPO (company pays 70%).
- Dental and vision premiums are covered at 100% for the employee and 50% for
  dependents.

## 3. Enrollment windows

- **New hires** have **30 days from their start date** to enroll. Missing the
  window means waiting for open enrollment.
- **Open enrollment** runs every year from **November 1 to November 15**,
  effective January 1.
- **Qualifying life events** (marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other
  coverage) open a **30-day special enrollment window** from the event date.
  Documentation is required.

## 4. 401(k)

- Eligible from day one; auto-enrollment at 4% unless declined.
- Company match: **100% of the first 4%** of salary contributed.
- Match vests immediately.

## 5. Life insurance and disability

- Company-paid life insurance at 1× annual salary for all benefits-eligible
  employees; supplemental coverage up to 4× available at employee cost.
- Short-term disability covers 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day
  waiting period.

## 6. Changing elections

Outside open enrollment and qualifying life events, elections cannot be
changed. Contact benefits@aurora.example for event verification.
