# Equipment Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-EQP-007 | Owner: IT | Last revised: 2026-02-15

## 1. Laptops

Every employee is issued one company-owned laptop, configured and
MDM-enrolled by IT before their start date. Standard configurations:
engineering roles receive a 16-inch, 32 GB machine; all other roles receive
a 14-inch, 16 GB machine. Exceptions require department-head approval.

Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if the device fails.
Employees never purchase laptops themselves; a personally purchased laptop
is not reimbursable (see Expense Policy POL-EXP-003 section 3) and may not
access production systems (see Data Security Policy POL-SEC-005 section 1).

## 2. Peripherals

One external monitor, keyboard, and mouse are provided for office desks.
Remote employees furnish their home office from the one-time USD 500
home-office stipend (Remote Work Policy POL-RW-002 section 5, claimed under
Expense Policy POL-EXP-003 section 3).

## 3. Mobile devices

Company phones are issued only for on-call rotations and field roles.
Personal phones used for work must have the MDM work profile and MFA
installed to access email or Slack.

## 4. Returns

All equipment must be returned within 10 days of employment ending, or upon
IT request during employment. IT provides a prepaid shipping label for
remote employees.

## 5. Lost or damaged equipment

Report loss or theft to IT and Security within 24 hours (Data Security
Policy POL-SEC-005 section 6). Accidental damage is covered by the company;
gross negligence may result in cost recovery consistent with local law.

## 6. Software

Only IT-approved software may be installed on company laptops. Requests go
through the IT service desk and are reviewed within 3 business days.

## 7. Requesting equipment

Standard equipment is issued automatically before the start date [POL-ONB-008
section 1]. Anything beyond the standard configuration — a second monitor, a
specific input device, a higher specification machine — is requested through
the IT service desk with the manager's approval. Requests under USD 300 are
approved by the line manager alone; above that, the department head approves.

Accessibility-related equipment is never charged to a team budget and does not
require a business justification. An employee needing an adjustment contacts
People Operations directly, and the request is handled confidentially.

## 8. Working with personal equipment

Personal laptops and desktops may not access production systems, customer data
or the internal network [POL-SEC-005 section 1]. Personal phones may access
email and chat only with the MDM work profile installed, which separates work
data and allows IT to wipe the work profile alone if the device is lost.

Where an employee prefers to use a personal peripheral — keyboard, mouse,
headset — that is permitted and is not reimbursed, since the company already
provides an equivalent.

## 9. Repairs and replacement

Faults are reported to the IT service desk. Devices under warranty are
repaired or replaced by the vendor; IT provides a loan machine where the
repair will take more than two working days. Remote employees are sent a loan
device by courier and return the faulty one in the same packaging.

Refresh happens every three years, or sooner if the device no longer supports
a supported operating system version. Employees are not expected to work on a
device that cannot receive security updates.

## 10. Disposal and data removal

Returned devices are wiped to the vendor's data-sanitisation standard before
reuse or disposal, and disposal is through a certified recycler. Employees do
not dispose of company equipment themselves, sell it, or give it away, even
where it is obsolete.

Employees may request to purchase their laptop at the end of its refresh cycle
at fair market value. The device is wiped and re-imaged before transfer, and
all company software licences are removed.

## 11. Home office setup

Remote and hybrid employees furnish their home workspace from the one-time
USD 500 stipend described in the Remote Work Policy [POL-RW-002 section 5] and
claimed under the Expense Policy [POL-EXP-003 section 3]. The stipend covers
a desk, chair, monitor arm, lighting and similar durable items. It does not
cover rent, utilities, internet service or coffee.

Items bought with the stipend belong to the employee, not to Aurora Dynamics,
and are not returned when the employee leaves. This is deliberate: tracking
and recovering low-value furniture across many countries costs more than the
furniture is worth, and the alternative — inventorying a colleague's spare
room — is intrusive.

The stipend is available once per employee rather than once per year. An
employee who moves house may request a second stipend after three years, and
People Operations approves these routinely rather than case by case.

Employees who need specific equipment for a health or accessibility reason are
covered separately under section 7 and are not asked to use the stipend for
it.

## 12. Internet and phone allowances

Fully remote employees receive USD 50 per month towards home internet, paid
with salary rather than claimed as an expense, so no receipts are needed.
Hybrid employees do not receive the allowance, since the office provides
connectivity on their office days.

Employees issued a company phone under section 3 have the line paid directly
by the company. Employees using a personal phone for work do not receive an
allowance; work use of a personal phone is expected to be light, and anyone
whose role requires heavy phone use should be issued a company device instead.

## 13. Shipping and international moves

IT ships equipment to the employee's registered home address. Where customs
duties apply, Aurora Dynamics pays them; employees should not accept charges
personally and should contact IT if a courier requests payment.

An employee relocating permanently to another country does not ship their
existing laptop themselves. IT arranges collection and issues a replacement
sourced locally, because warranty and support are country-specific and a
machine bought in one region often cannot be serviced in another.

Temporary travel with company equipment needs no approval within the region
the device was issued in. Travel outside it is covered by the Data Security
Policy [POL-SEC-005 section 4], which sets out the encryption and border
rules.
