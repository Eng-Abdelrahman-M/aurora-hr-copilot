# Data Security Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-SEC-005 · Owner: Security Engineering · Last revised: 2026-03-01

## 1. Devices

- Only **company-issued, MDM-enrolled devices** may access production systems,
  customer data, or the corporate VPN.
- Personal devices may access email and Slack only through the mobile apps
  with MFA enabled; storing customer data on personal devices is prohibited.
- Full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock (max 5 minutes) are enforced
  by MDM and must not be disabled.

## 2. Authentication

- **MFA is mandatory** for all company accounts.
- Passwords must be generated and stored in the company password manager.
- Sharing credentials is prohibited, including with other employees.

## 3. Networks and remote access

- When working outside a company office, connect through the **company VPN**
  before accessing internal systems.
- **Public Wi-Fi** (cafés, airports, hotels) may be used **only with the VPN
  active**; captive portals may be completed before connecting the VPN.
- Home routers must use WPA2 or better with a non-default password.

## 4. Working while traveling or abroad

- Devices must never be left unattended in public places or checked luggage.
- For trips abroad longer than 30 days, notify Security Engineering so export
  control and device posture can be reviewed (see Remote Work Policy
  POL-RW-002 section 3).
- In high-risk countries (list maintained by Security), loaner laptops are
  issued instead of primary devices.

## 5. Data handling

- Customer data lives only in approved systems of record; exporting it to
  spreadsheets or personal storage is prohibited.
- Confidential documents are shared via access-controlled links, never as
  attachments to external parties.

## 6. Incident reporting

Report lost devices, suspected phishing, or any data exposure to
security@aurora.example **within 24 hours** of discovery. Early reporting is
never penalized; late reporting may be.

## 7. Access reviews and least privilege

Access to production systems, customer data and finance systems is granted on
a least-privilege basis: an employee receives the narrowest access that lets
them do their job, and access is attached to a role rather than to a person.
Managers review the access held by their direct reports every quarter and
confirm in writing that it is still required. Access not confirmed within
fifteen business days of the review opening is revoked automatically.

Requests for additional access go through the IT service desk with the
manager's approval recorded. Temporary elevated access — for an incident, a
migration or an audit — is granted for a fixed window not exceeding seven days
and expires without further action.

When an employee changes role, their previous access is removed on the
effective date of the change rather than being allowed to accumulate. On
termination, all access is revoked within two hours of the leaving time
recorded by People Operations.

## 8. Passwords and credential handling

Passwords must be unique per service and at least sixteen characters, or
generated by the company password manager, which every employee is enrolled in
during onboarding [POL-ONB-008 section 2]. Credentials are never shared
between people, never sent over chat or email, and never stored in code,
spreadsheets or ticket descriptions.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory on every system that supports it.
Hardware security keys are issued to employees with production or finance
access and must be used for those systems; SMS-based second factors are not
accepted for privileged access.

A credential that has been exposed — pasted into the wrong window, committed
to a repository, or entered on a suspicious page — must be rotated
immediately, and the exposure reported under section 6 even if the employee
believes no harm resulted. Reporting a mistake promptly is treated as correct
behaviour and is never itself grounds for disciplinary action.

## 9. Artificial intelligence and third-party tools

Employees may use approved AI assistants for drafting, summarising and coding.
Customer data, personal data of colleagues, credentials, unreleased financial
figures and source code from private repositories must not be entered into any
tool that has not been approved for that data class. The approved tool list is
maintained by IT and published in the internal handbook.

Adopting a new third-party tool that will process company or customer data
requires a security review before purchase, regardless of price — including
free tiers. Free tools are not exempt: the review exists to check data
handling, not cost. Requests go to IT with a description of the data involved,
and most reviews complete within five business days.

Output from an AI tool is the responsibility of the employee who uses it. It
must be checked before being sent to a customer, committed to a repository or
relied on for a decision.

## 10. Physical security

Office doors, server cupboards and storage rooms are badge-controlled.
Employees do not hold doors open for people they do not recognise, and
visitors are signed in and escorted. Laptops are not left unattended in public
spaces, and screens are locked whenever the employee steps away, including at
home where others share the space.

Printed material containing customer or personal data is collected from the
printer immediately and shredded when no longer needed.
