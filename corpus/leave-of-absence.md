# Leave of Absence Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-LOA-006 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-02-10

## 1. Parental leave

- **16 weeks fully paid** for all new parents (birth, adoption, foster
  placement) with at least 6 months of service.
- Leave may be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the event.
- Notify People Operations at least **60 days** before the expected start
  where practicable.

## 2. Medical leave

- Up to **12 weeks** of job-protected medical leave per rolling 12 months,
  run concurrently with FMLA where applicable.
- Short-term disability (see Benefits Guide POL-BEN-004 section 5) provides
  income replacement after the waiting period.
- A medical certification is required within 15 days of the request.

## 3. Bereavement

- **5 days paid** for immediate family (spouse/partner, child, parent,
  sibling, grandparent); **2 days paid** for extended family.
- Additional unpaid days may be approved by your manager.

## 4. Jury duty and civic leave

- Paid leave for the duration of jury service; provide the summons to your
  manager and People Operations.
- Up to 4 hours paid to vote where polls are not open outside working hours.

## 5. Unpaid personal leave

- After 12 months of service, employees may request up to **30 days** unpaid
  personal leave, subject to department-head approval and business needs.
- Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave of 30 days or fewer.

## 6. Returning from leave

Employees returning from a protected leave are restored to the same or an
equivalent role. Contact People Operations two weeks before your return date
to coordinate re-onboarding and system access.

## 7. Requesting a leave of absence

Foreseeable leave — parental leave, planned surgery, a sabbatical — is
requested at least 30 days before the intended start date, through People
Operations rather than through the PTO system. The request states the intended
start and end dates and, where relevant, the expected pattern of return.

Unforeseeable leave is requested as soon as the employee is able. Nobody is
expected to file paperwork during a medical emergency or a bereavement; a
message to the manager or People Operations is enough at the time, and the
formal record is completed afterwards.

People Operations confirms in writing what is approved, which portion is paid,
how benefits are treated, and the agreed contact arrangements during the
absence. Managers are told the dates and nothing more: medical details are
never shared with the manager or the team.

## 8. Pay during leave

Parental leave is paid at 100% of base salary for the paid portion set out in
section 1. Medical leave is paid at 100% for the first eight weeks and 70%
thereafter, up to the statutory maximum where local law provides one. Where a
state or national scheme pays a benefit, Aurora Dynamics tops it up to the
percentages above rather than paying in addition.

Bereavement and jury duty leave are paid in full. Unpaid personal leave is, as
the name says, unpaid; the employee's benefits are treated under the Benefits
Guide [POL-BEN-009 section 10].

Bonus and equity vesting continue during any paid leave. During unpaid leave
longer than 90 days, equity vesting pauses and resumes on return, extending
the vesting end date by the length of the pause.

## 9. Contact during leave and keeping-in-touch days

Employees on leave are not expected to read email, attend meetings or respond
to messages, and colleagues should not contact them about work. The employee
nominates a preferred contact method for essential administrative matters
only — payroll, benefits, or the return date.

An employee on parental or long-term medical leave may work up to ten
keeping-in-touch days without ending the leave or reducing pay. These are
voluntary on both sides, arranged in advance, and paid at normal salary.

## 10. Phased and delayed return

A phased return — reduced hours building back to full over an agreed period —
is available after any leave of more than eight weeks, at full pay for the
agreed reduced hours, for up to four weeks. Longer phased returns are possible
by agreement with People Operations.

Where an employee is not able to return on the agreed date, they tell People
Operations before that date so the leave can be extended. An unexplained
failure to return is handled as an absence matter rather than automatically as
a resignation, and People Operations will attempt to make contact through the
nominated method before taking any further step.
