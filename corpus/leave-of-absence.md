# Leave of Absence Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-LOA-006 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-02-10

## 1. Parental leave

- **16 weeks fully paid** for all new parents (birth, adoption, foster
  placement) with at least 6 months of service.
- Leave may be taken in up to two blocks within 12 months of the event.
- Notify People Operations at least **60 days** before the expected start
  where practicable.

## 2. Medical leave

- Up to **12 weeks** of job-protected medical leave per rolling 12 months,
  run concurrently with FMLA where applicable.
- Short-term disability (see Benefits Guide POL-BEN-004 section 5) provides
  income replacement after the waiting period.
- A medical certification is required within 15 days of the request.

## 3. Bereavement

- **5 days paid** for immediate family (spouse/partner, child, parent,
  sibling, grandparent); **2 days paid** for extended family.
- Additional unpaid days may be approved by your manager.

## 4. Jury duty and civic leave

- Paid leave for the duration of jury service; provide the summons to your
  manager and People Operations.
- Up to 4 hours paid to vote where polls are not open outside working hours.

## 5. Unpaid personal leave

- After 12 months of service, employees may request up to **30 days** unpaid
  personal leave, subject to department-head approval and business needs.
- Benefits continue during approved unpaid leave of 30 days or fewer.

## 6. Returning from leave

Employees returning from a protected leave are restored to the same or an
equivalent role. Contact People Operations two weeks before your return date
to coordinate re-onboarding and system access.
