# Onboarding Guide — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-ONB-009 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-02-20

## 1. Before day one

- Sign your offer paperwork and complete identity verification in the HR
  portal at least 3 business days before your start date.
- IT ships your laptop to arrive by your start date (see Equipment Policy
  POL-EQP-007). Remote hires should confirm their shipping address.

## 2. First day

- Complete I-9 / right-to-work verification.
- Activate your accounts: SSO, email, Slack, HR portal. Enroll in MFA and the
  password manager (mandatory — see Data Security Policy POL-SEC-005).
- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned by your manager.

## 3. First week

- Complete required trainings: Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, and
  Anti-Harassment (all due within 7 days).
- **Benefits enrollment**: you have 30 days from your start date — see the
  Benefits Guide (POL-BEN-004 section 3). Book the optional benefits
  orientation call.
- Review the PTO Policy (POL-PTO-001); your vacation accrual started on day
  one.
- Set up your home office if remote; the $500 stipend is claimable from day
  one under the Expense Policy (POL-EXP-003 section 3).

## 4. First month

- Agree written 30/60/90-day goals with your manager by the end of week 2.
- Complete role-specific compliance training assigned in the LMS.
- Verify your payroll, tax withholding, and emergency contacts in the portal.

## 5. Probation

The first 90 days are an evaluation period with a formal check-in at day 45
and day 90. Probation does not limit access to benefits or PTO accrual.
