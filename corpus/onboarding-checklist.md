# Onboarding Guide — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-ONB-009 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2026-02-20

## 1. Before day one

- Sign your offer paperwork and complete identity verification in the HR
  portal at least 3 business days before your start date.
- IT ships your laptop to arrive by your start date (see Equipment Policy
  POL-EQP-007). Remote hires should confirm their shipping address.

## 2. First day

- Complete I-9 / right-to-work verification.
- Activate your accounts: SSO, email, Slack, HR portal. Enroll in MFA and the
  password manager (mandatory — see Data Security Policy POL-SEC-005).
- Meet your onboarding buddy, assigned by your manager.

## 3. First week

- Complete required trainings: Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, and
  Anti-Harassment (all due within 7 days).
- **Benefits enrollment**: you have 30 days from your start date — see the
  Benefits Guide (POL-BEN-004 section 3). Book the optional benefits
  orientation call.
- Review the PTO Policy (POL-PTO-001); your vacation accrual started on day
  one.
- Set up your home office if remote; the $500 stipend is claimable from day
  one under the Expense Policy (POL-EXP-003 section 3).

## 4. First month

- Agree written 30/60/90-day goals with your manager by the end of week 2.
- Complete role-specific compliance training assigned in the LMS.
- Verify your payroll, tax withholding, and emergency contacts in the portal.

## 5. Probation

The first 90 days are an evaluation period with a formal check-in at day 45
and day 90. Probation does not limit access to benefits or PTO accrual.

## 6. Manager responsibilities

The hiring manager confirms the start date and equipment needs with IT and
People Operations at least ten business days before the start date, so that
the laptop is configured and shipped in time [POL-EQP-007 section 1].

Before day one the manager writes a 30/60/90 plan describing what the new
joiner is expected to learn, contribute and own by each point, and nominates
an onboarding buddy from the team who is not the manager. The buddy is the
person for the questions that feel too small to ask a manager.

In the first week the manager holds a one-to-one on the first day and again at
the end of the week, and ensures the new joiner has been introduced to every
team they will work with. Weekly one-to-ones continue for at least the first
three months.

## 7. Required training

Every new joiner completes four modules within their first 30 days: data
security and acceptable use, code of conduct and anti-harassment, privacy and
data handling, and an introduction to the products. Completion is tracked by
People Operations and is a condition of passing probation [section 5].

Employees with production access complete an additional incident-response
module before that access is granted [POL-SEC-005 section 7]. Everyone repeats
the security and conduct modules annually; the annual cycle opens in October
and closes at the end of November.

## 8. Access and accounts

Accounts are created before the start date but activate on the morning of day
one. The standard set is email, chat, the HR system, the document store and
the team's project tools. Anything beyond that is requested by the manager
through the IT service desk, following least privilege [POL-SEC-005 section 7].

The new joiner enrols in the password manager and sets up multi-factor
authentication on day one, before accessing anything else. Employees who will
hold production or finance access are issued hardware security keys, which are
shipped with the laptop.

## 9. Remote and international joiners

Remote joiners receive the same equipment and the same onboarding, with the
first-day introductions held by video and the buddy pairing chosen with time
zone overlap in mind. Where possible, remote joiners visit an office within
their first three months; the trip is arranged and funded by the company under
the Expense Policy [POL-EXP-003 section 4].

Joiners outside the countries where Aurora Dynamics is registered as an
employer are engaged through an employer of record. Their benefits and leave
follow local law and their contract rather than this handbook, and People
Operations confirms the differences in writing during their first week.

## 10. Offboarding

When an employee leaves, the manager tells People Operations as soon as the
leaving date is known. Access is revoked at the leaving time [POL-SEC-005
section 7], equipment is returned within ten days [POL-EQP-007 section 4], and
the final pay run settles any accrued vacation balance [POL-PTO-001 section
11].

People Operations offers an exit conversation to every leaver. It is optional,
confidential, and summarised to the business only in aggregate.
