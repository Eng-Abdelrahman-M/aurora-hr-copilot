Company Holidays 2026 — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-HOL-010 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2025-12-01

1. Observed holidays (US)

Aurora Dynamics observes 11 paid company holidays in 2026:

  • New Year's Day — Thursday, January 1
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Monday, January 19
  • Presidents' Day — Monday, February 16
  • Memorial Day — Monday, May 25
  • Juneteenth — Friday, June 19
  • Independence Day (observed) — Friday, July 3
  • Labor Day — Monday, September 7
  • Thanksgiving — Thursday, November 26
  • Day after Thanksgiving — Friday, November 27
  • Christmas Eve — Thursday, December 24
  • Christmas Day — Friday, December 25

2. Floating holidays

Each employee receives 2 floating holidays per year, usable for any cultural, religious, or personal observance. Floating holidays do not carry over and require the same notice as a 1-day PTO request (see PTO Policy POL-PTO-001 section 3).

3. Holidays outside the US

Employees outside the US observe their local statutory holiday calendar, published by their local People Operations partner each December. The two floating holidays apply globally.

4. Working on a holiday

Non-exempt employees who are required to work on a company holiday receive 1.5× pay for hours worked plus a substitute day off, to be used within 90 days.

5. Holidays during PTO

A company holiday falling inside an approved PTO block is not deducted from the employee's vacation balance.

6. Regional holidays

Employees observe the holiday calendar of the country in which they are payrolled, not the calendar of the office they report to. The company-wide list in section 2 applies to all US-payrolled employees; employees in Germany, Portugal and the United Kingdom follow the published national list for their country, which People Operations circulates each December for the year ahead. Regional lists are the same length or longer than the US list; nobody receives fewer paid holidays because of where they are payrolled.

Where a national holiday falls on a weekend, the substitute day follows local practice rather than a company rule. Employees who are unsure which list applies to them should ask People Operations rather than assume.

7. Religious and cultural observance

An employee may swap up to three company holidays for days of religious or cultural significance to them, keeping the total number of paid days unchanged. The swap is requested through People Operations at least fifteen business days in advance and does not need the manager's approval, though the manager is told the dates so that coverage can be arranged.

Floating holidays (section 3) may also be used for observance, and are the simpler route where the employee does not wish to explain the reason. No employee is ever required to state the nature of an observance.

8. Office closure between Christmas and New Year

Aurora Dynamics offices close from 24 December to 1 January inclusive. These days are paid and are not deducted from the vacation balance, and they are in addition to the holidays listed in section 2.

Teams that must maintain coverage during the closure - support, on-call engineering - arrange a rota in advance. Employees who work during the closure receive substitute days off under the same terms as section 4, to be taken within 90 days.

9. Holiday calendar changes

The calendar for the following year is published by 1 November. Once published it does not change, except where a government declares an additional public holiday, in which case People Operations circulates an update within five business days.

Requests to move a company holiday for an individual team are not granted; individual swaps under section 7 are the mechanism for personal preference.

10. Part-time and pro-rated holidays

Part-time employees receive company holidays that fall on days they normally work, at their normal pay for that day. Where a part-time employee's working pattern means they miss a disproportionate number of holidays - for example someone who never works Mondays, when several holidays fall - People Operations grants substitute days so that the pro-rated entitlement is matched over the year. Employees do not need to request this; it is calculated in December and credited automatically.

11. Holidays and the PTO balance

Company holidays are separate from vacation and are never deducted from the vacation balance, whether the employee is working that week or on approved leave (section 5). Floating holidays are separate again: they are granted annually, do not accrue monthly, and expire at year end without payout. See the PTO Policy POL-PTO-001 section 5 for carryover treatment of vacation days, which is different.

An employee who has exhausted their vacation balance may still take unpaid time around a holiday period with manager approval, subject to the blackout rules in POL-PTO-001 section 6.

12. On-call during holidays

Employees on an on-call rota over a company holiday receive the substitute day described in section 4 whether or not they are actually paged. Being available is the burden being compensated, not the interruption.

On-call rotas covering the year-end closure (section 8) are published by 1 November so that people can plan. No employee is placed on the year-end rota in two consecutive years without their agreement.
