Company Holidays 2026 — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-HOL-010 · Owner: People Operations · Last revised: 2025-12-01

1. Observed holidays (US)

Aurora Dynamics observes 11 paid company holidays in 2026:

  • New Year's Day — Thursday, January 1
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day — Monday, January 19
  • Presidents' Day — Monday, February 16
  • Memorial Day — Monday, May 25
  • Juneteenth — Friday, June 19
  • Independence Day (observed) — Friday, July 3
  • Labor Day — Monday, September 7
  • Thanksgiving — Thursday, November 26
  • Day after Thanksgiving — Friday, November 27
  • Christmas Eve — Thursday, December 24
  • Christmas Day — Friday, December 25

2. Floating holidays

Each employee receives 2 floating holidays per year, usable for any cultural, religious, or personal observance. Floating holidays do not carry over and require the same notice as a 1-day PTO request (see PTO Policy POL-PTO-001 section 3).

3. Holidays outside the US

Employees outside the US observe their local statutory holiday calendar, published by their local People Operations partner each December. The two floating holidays apply globally.

4. Working on a holiday

Non-exempt employees who are required to work on a company holiday receive 1.5× pay for hours worked plus a substitute day off, to be used within 90 days.

5. Holidays during PTO

A company holiday falling inside an approved PTO block is not deducted from the employee's vacation balance.
