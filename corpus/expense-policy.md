# Expense & Reimbursement Policy — Aurora Dynamics

Document ID: POL-EXP-003 · Owner: Finance · Last revised: 2026-01-20

## 1. General rules

- Expenses must be **business-related, reasonable, and documented**.
- Itemized receipts are required for any expense of **$25 or more**.
- Submit expenses in the finance portal within **60 days** of the purchase
  date. Late submissions require CFO approval.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll cycle after approval.

## 2. Approval thresholds

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $250 | Manager |
| $250.01 – $2,000 | Manager + Department head |
| Over $2,000 | Department head + Finance |

## 3. Home office and equipment

- Remote and hybrid employees may use the **one-time $500 home-office
  stipend** (see Remote Work Policy POL-RW-002) for desks, chairs, monitors,
  keyboards, and similar peripherals.
- A **home office chair is reimbursable only from the $500 stipend**; there is
  no separate chair budget.
- **Laptops are not reimbursable as an expense.** Computers are issued and
  owned by the company through the Equipment Policy (POL-EQP-007). Buying a
  laptop personally and expensing it will be rejected.
- Monitors above the stipend limit require department-head approval.

## 4. Travel

- Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy allowed for longer
  flights.
- Hotel cap: **$220/night** in standard cities, **$320/night** in high-cost
  cities (list maintained by Finance).
- Meals while traveling: up to **$75/day** with receipts; alcohol is not
  reimbursable.
- Mileage for personal-car business travel is reimbursed at the prevailing
  IRS rate.

## 5. Software and subscriptions

- Individual software subscriptions require manager approval **before**
  purchase and must go through IT for security review if they process company
  data.

## 6. Non-reimbursable items

Commuting to your assigned office, parking fines, personal entertainment,
home internet upgrades beyond the stipend, and any expense without a receipt
over $25.
